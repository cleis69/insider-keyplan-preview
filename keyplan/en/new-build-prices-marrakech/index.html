Methodology

These figures are calculated by KEYPLAN from the development listings on this site. They describe the listed developments, not the Marrakech property market as a whole.

  • Developments counted: every new development listed by KEYPLAN in the neighbourhood, whether at launch stage, under construction or delivered. The listing of a delivered development stays online, with the status “Delivered”, and it stays counted.
  • “From”: the lowest starting price shown on the listing of a development in that neighbourhood, in Moroccan dirhams (MAD).
  • Average price per m²: simple average of the average prices per m² shown on the listings of the neighbourhood's developments, not weighted by number of units, rounded to the nearest dirham.
  • These are asking prices shown on the listings, not recorded sale prices.
  • The neighbourhood is the one shown on the listing, and each development is counted in one neighbourhood only.

How KEYPLAN checks developments: the TRUST index

Before comparing two prices

Two prices per m² are only comparable if they cover the same floor area and the same specification. Put these questions to the developer, in writing:

  • Does the price per m² apply to living area, built area or another measure?
  • What does the price include (finishes, parking, garden, pool) and which costs are added on top?
  • Is the price guaranteed in the reservation contract, and until what date?
