How the ranking works

A developer's score is the average, rounded to the nearest whole number, of the scores of its developments that have one. Each development is scored out of 100 on the TRUST grid. The ranking only covers developers listed by KEYPLAN, not every developer in Marrakech.

  • Five criteria, worth 100 points in all: land title (25), track record (15), urban planning (20), off-plan safeguards (25) and transparency (15). “Off-plan” means buying before the property is built (VEFA, vente en l'état futur d'achèvement, under Moroccan law).
  • Each criterion is made of items: a verified item earns its full points, a partly verified item half of them, an item not verified or not disclosed none.
  • Three items are vital: land title, building or subdivision permit and completion or refund guarantee. If any of them is not verified (partly verified is not enough), the development has no score: “Incomplete file”.
  • Tiers: 80 to 100, “Excellent file”; 65 to 79, “Good file”; 0 to 64, “File to be completed”.
  • The last check date is the most recent of the developer's own record and those of its developments.

How scores are checked

Every score goes through the same steps, from the pre-score sent to the developer to the quarterly review.

  1. 1Pre-score. The score is worked out item by item, from the documents checked on the TRUST grid, then sent to the developer.
  2. 215 days to reply. The developer can reply within this period, before anything is published.
  3. 3Publication. The score is published, together with the developer's reply as written if there is one.
  4. 4Reviewed every quarter. Published scores are reviewed again; each row of the ranking shows its last check date.
