Developer ranking 2026

A developer's score is read through the scores of its developments.

The TRUST method
  • The developer's score is the average of its scored developments. Rounded to a whole number; a development with an incomplete file is not counted. No scored development: no score, the developer has an incomplete file.
  • Three items are checked on the developer. Delivered developments, delivery deadlines met, documents provided on request: they count in the score of each of its developments.
  • Three vital items are checked on each development. Land title, building or subdivision permit, completion or refund guarantee: if any one is not verified, the development has no score.
  • A right of reply published as written. The developer receives a pre-score and has 15 days to reply; the reply appears on its page. Every file is reviewed each quarter.
