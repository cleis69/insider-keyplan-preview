Comparing developments of the same type

Three reference points help set similar developments side by side:

  • What the announced price covers: are the plot and villa surfaces, garden, pool and finishes included? Two prices are only comparable on the same scope.
  • The level of verification: the TRUST rating shows, for each development, whether the land title, the building permit and the VEFA guarantee are verified, unverified or not disclosed.
  • Progress: the status (launch phase, under construction, delivered) and the announced delivery appear on each line of the list above.

Understanding an off-plan purchase

In Morocco, buying off-plan is normally a VEFA sale (vente en l'état futur d'achèvement, the sale of a property yet to be completed).
