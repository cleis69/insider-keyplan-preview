The TRUST method in detail
  • The surface used. Living area or including common areas: it weighs on the price per m², as do the floor of the unit and the planned service charges.
  • The level of verification. The TRUST score, out of 100, shows item by item what is verified for each development; without a verified land title, building or subdivision permit and completion or refund guarantee, there is no score.
  • Progress. The status (launch, under construction, delivered) and the announced completion appear on each card above.

Understanding an off-plan purchase

In Morocco, buying off-plan is normally a VEFA sale (vente en l'état futur d'achèvement, law 107-12): the buyer pays before the property is built.
