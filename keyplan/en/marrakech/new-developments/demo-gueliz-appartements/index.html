The TRUST file, item by item

The score out of 100 adds up the points of eleven items, grouped into five criteria. A verified item earns its full points, a partly verified item half, an item not verified or not disclosed none; the total is rounded to the nearest whole number. Items marked “Developer” are verified once for the developer and count for each of its developments.

Land title, building or subdivision permit and completion or refund guarantee are vital items: if any one is not verified, the development has no score. VEFA is buying off-plan (law 107-12): the buyer pays before the property exists, hence the weight given to the guarantee.
