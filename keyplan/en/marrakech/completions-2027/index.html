How to read this page

Dates are the completion dates announced on the development listings: an announced date remains a forecast until the development is delivered.

  • Q1 to Q4 are the four quarters of the year: Q1 is January to March, Q2 April to June, Q3 July to September, Q4 October to December.
  • A development is placed under the quarter shown on its listing. With no quarter given, it appears under “Quarter to be confirmed”.
  • A delivered development stays in the list, with the status “Delivered”.
  • Within a quarter, developments are sorted by TRUST rating, then alphabetically.

Questions to ask before relying on a completion date

Put these questions to the developer, then to the notary, before relying on a date:

  • Is the completion date written into the contract, and what does the contract say if it slips?
  • How far along is the building work today, backed by a dated document?
  • Which guarantee covers completion of the development, and who issues it?
