How the ranking works

A developer's rating is the lowest of its developments' ratings, and each development is rated on the TRUST grid. The ranking only covers developers listed by KEYPLAN, not every developer in Marrakech.

  • A development's land title, building or subdivision permit and off-plan safeguards must all be verified (VEFA, short for vente en l'état futur d'achèvement, is the sale of a property before it is built). Otherwise the assessment is incomplete, and the development is never rated A or B.
  • A development is rated A when the developer's track record and transparency are verified too, and B otherwise.
  • A developer with no listed development stays at incomplete assessment.
  • The last check date is the most recent of the developer's own record and those of its developments.
