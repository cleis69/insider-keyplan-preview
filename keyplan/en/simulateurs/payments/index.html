How it is calculated
  • The price is paid in three stages: on reservation, during construction and on handover. The three shares always add up to 100%: the construction share is what is left.
  • During construction, the payments are equal. In a contract, they are often called “payment calls”.
  • Each amount is rounded to the dirham; any difference goes on the last payment, so that the total is exactly the price.
  • With your dates, the construction payments fall at regular intervals: an assumption. The contract sets the actual due dates, often tied to construction stages.
  • Starting split: the development's published schedule when it follows this pattern; otherwise an example, to be replaced by the schedule in your contract.
  • No rate, no costs and no currency conversion are added to the price.

Good to know: registration duty on an off-plan purchase

Good to know: registration duty on an off-plan purchase

It is not paid in step with the instalments: a small fixed duty on reservation, the rest with the final sale deed.

  • Reservation contract, preliminary contract

    Each is registered for a fixed duty of 200 MAD; the proportional duty is due on the final sale deed.

    Source: General Tax Code 2026 (new tab), CGI, art. 135-II-16°, checked on 9 October 2026

  • Final sale deed

    Registration duty of 4% of the price stated in the deed, VAT included, plus 2% on any part paid in cash or without payment references in the deed when the price exceeds 300,000 MAD (deeds drawn up since 1 July 2026).

    Source: General Tax Code 2026 (new tab), CGI, art. 133-I-F-1°, checked on 9 October 2026

    Source: General Tax Code 2026 (new tab), CGI, art. 133-III (loi de finances n° 50-25), checked on 9 October 2026
