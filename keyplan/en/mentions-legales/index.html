The text, data, plans, illustrations and structure of KEYPLAN are protected. Any full or partial reproduction or reuse requires our prior written consent.

Content and liability

The information published about developments (prices, floor areas, schedules, plans, status) comes from developers and from the documents we have consulted. It is indicative, valid on the last-checked date shown on each page, and may change without notice.

The TRUST score is an editorial assessment based on the documents consulted on the date shown. It is not legal, tax or financial advice, nor a guarantee, and it does not replace a notary's review of the file before any commitment.
