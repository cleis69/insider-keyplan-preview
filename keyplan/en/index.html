TRUSTHow the score is worked out

Illustrative photo · Miltiadis Fragkidis / Unsplash

What KEYPLAN checks on every development

  • Verified land title

    Title established, no registered dispute or charge. A vital item: without it, no score.

  • Building permit

    Building or subdivision permit, checked against the document. A vital item.

  • Off-plan guarantee

    Completion or refund guarantee, under law 107-12. A vital item.

  • Verification date

    Every score is dated and reviewed each quarter.

A score out of 100 that shows its evidence, item by item.

Read TRUST like a vehicle inspection: eleven items across five criteria, each checked against a document, with the date of the last check. Before publication, the developer receives a pre-score and has 15 days to reply; the reply is published as written.

  • TLand title 25 pointsLand title established, no registered dispute or charge.Vital item: land title registered in the name of the developer or its project company.
  • RTrack record 15 pointsDelivered developments, delivery deadlines met.
  • UUrban planning 20 pointsBuilding or subdivision permit, development matching it.Vital item: building or subdivision permit obtained.
  • SOff-plan safeguards 25 pointsCompliant preliminary contract, completion or refund guarantee.Vital item: completion guarantee or guarantee of refund of the sums paid.
  • TTransparency 15 pointsPublished plans, specification and prices, documents provided on request.

Without its three vital items verified, a development has no score: it shows “Incomplete file”.

How to read the score

Score out of 100, five weighted criteria.

See the method
80–100Excellent file
Score from 80 to 100. All three vital items are verified.
65–79Good file
Score from 65 to 79. All three vital items are verified.
0–64File to be completed
Score from 0 to 64. All three vital items are verified; other items are still to be verified.
Incomplete file
At least one vital item (land title, building or subdivision permit, completion or refund guarantee) is not verified: no score.
