Property simulators: what would you like to know?

Three simple questions to compare new-build developments on the same basis. Pick yours: a few sliders are enough, and every result says where its figures come from.

Updated on 9 October 2026
  • Payments

    How much will I pay, and when?

    The amount to pay on reservation, during construction and on handover of an off-plan purchase.

    You need: the property price.

    Start
  • Financing

    How much will my financing cost each month?

    The monthly payment, purchase costs and total cost, with a conventional loan or participative financing (Murabaha, Ijara).

    You need: the price, your down payment and the rate offered by your bank.

    Start
  • Yield

    How much could this property earn me?

    Gross and net yield, and what is left each month, from your own rent and cost assumptions.

    You need: the price and the rent you expect.

    Start

How these simulators work

How these simulators work

  • Sourced parameters

    Registration duty, land registry, VAT, stamp duty: every legal value comes from an official text, shows its source and the date it was checked, and can be changed.

  • Nothing invented

    When no text sets a value (notary fees, insurance, rents, management fees), the field stays empty: ask your notary or your bank, or enter your own assumption.

  • One development, three calculations

    Choose a development in one tool: its starting price follows you from tab to tab during your visit. You can always replace it with the price of a unit.

Indicative results, calculated from your entries and the sourced parameters shown under each field. This is neither a loan or financing offer nor legal, tax or financial advice: the contract, the notary, the bank and a tax adviser prevail.

Amounts in Moroccan dirhams, with no currency conversion. Nothing you enter is sent to KEYPLAN: the calculation runs in your browser, which keeps your latest figures for the session so you can move from one tool to another.
