How it is calculated
Gross annual rent
monthly rent × 12 (let all year round)
Rent collected
gross annual rent − vacancy (monthly rent × months without a tenant)
Costs and management
service charges and upkeep + insurance + other costs + municipal services tax (rate × rent collected) + management (rate × rent collected)
Net income before tax
rent collected − costs − management (vacancy already deducted)
Total purchase cost
purchase price + purchase costs + furnishing
Gross yield
gross annual rent ÷ purchase price
Net yield
net income before tax ÷ total purchase cost
Tax on rent, on the scale
income tax on (your other income + taxable base) − income tax on your other income; taxable base = rent collected × (1 − allowance); income tax = income × bracket rate − amount to deduct. The allowance is a flat rate: neither actual costs nor loan interest are deducted.
Net yield after tax
(net income before tax − tax) ÷ total purchase cost
What is left each month (cash flow)
(net income, before or after tax − loan payments over a year) ÷ 12; with no loan, the payments are zero. Capital repayment is included: it is what goes in and out of your account.
Rounding
annual amounts rounded to the dirham, totals made of rounded amounts; yields to a hundredth of a point; monthly amounts to the dirham.

The figures of the result, explained

The big figure is the net yield. The other three complete it: the gross yield, the yield after tax and what is left each month once everything is paid.

  • Net yield

    Rent collected after vacancy, minus costs, the municipal services tax and management, divided by the total purchase cost: price, purchase costs and furnishing.

  • Gross yield

    Gross annual rent divided by the purchase price, as if the property were let all year round, with no costs. Useful to compare properties, not to estimate what you will receive.

  • Yield after tax

    The net yield after tax on rent, under the regime you choose. An empty tax rate leaves this yield uncalculated rather than at zero.

  • What is left each month (cash flow)

    Net income for the year, before or after tax, minus the loan payments, divided by twelve. It includes capital repayment: it is what goes in and out of your account.

Good to know: how rent is taxed

These parameters prefill the simulator; each one can be changed.

  • Year-round letting: allowance, then the scale

    Rent collected gets a flat allowance of 40%; the rest is added to your other income taxable in Morocco and follows the income tax scale. Actual costs and loan interest are not deducted on top.

    Source: Tax Directorate (DGI), 2026 tax guide for Moroccans abroad (new tab), CGI, art. 64-II, checked on 9 October 2026

    Source: Tax Directorate (DGI), 2026 tax guide for Moroccans abroad (new tab), CGI, art. 73-I (loi de finances n° 60-24), checked on 9 October 2026

  • Company or professional tenant

    The tenant withholds tax at source: 10% below 120,000 MAD of gross annual rent, 15% above, credited against your annual tax. You can also opt for final taxation at 20% of gross rent.

    Source: Tax Directorate (DGI), 2026 tax guide for Moroccans abroad (new tab), CGI, art. 73-II et 160 bis, checked on 9 October 2026

    Source: Tax Directorate (DGI), 2026 tax guide for Moroccans abroad (new tab), CGI, art. 64-IV et 73-II-F-12° (loi de finances n° 60-24), checked on 9 October 2026

  • Municipal services tax

    For a let property, it is calculated on the rent: 10.5% within an urban perimeter, 6.5% in other areas covered by a zoning plan.

    Source: Tax Directorate (DGI), municipal services tax (new tab), Law no. 47-06, art. 33 to 36 (Laws 07-20 and 14-25), checked on 9 October 2026

  • Short-term letting

    An operating licence is required and the tourist tax applies; no official text yet states how this income is taxed. The tax rate is therefore yours to enter, with a tax adviser's help.

    To be confirmed with a tax adviser: no official text states whether this income is rental or business income, nor the VAT that applies.

    Source: Morocco national portal, tourist accommodation orders (new tab), Law no. 80-14; Decree no. 2-23-441; implementing orders announced, checked on 9 October 2026

What the simulator does not do

  • It prefills no rent, no nightly rate, no occupancy and no costs: these are market data, specific to each property.
  • It counts no rent increase, no resale, no capital gain and no tax on resale.
  • It converts no currency: all amounts are in dirhams.
