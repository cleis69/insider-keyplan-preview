The data we process

  • What you send us. If you write to us or call us (email, phone, messaging), we process what you tell us: your identity, contact details, message and details of your purchase project.
  • Technical data. Like any web server, the host records your IP address, the date and the pages requested in its logs, to run and secure the site.
  • Audience measurement. None: this site installs no audience-measurement or advertising tool.

Why we process it

  • to answer your request and, if you wish, to guide you through your purchase;
  • to run and secure the site.

We process this data because you sent us a request or with your consent. Audience measurement is only switched on with your consent.
