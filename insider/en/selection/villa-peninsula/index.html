92 two-storey villas, each with a private pool and roof terrace, in a gated residence on the Route d'Amizmiz.

Overview

Villa Peninsula is a residence of 92 villas developed by OCRE BY SASY SARL on the Route d'Amizmiz. The developer places it less than 15 minutes from central Marrakech and 10 minutes from the airport, with views of the Atlas. Two models are offered, both on two floors with a private pool and an accessible roof terrace: 60 Oasis villas of 238 m² with three suites, and 32 Atlas villas of 262 m² with four suites, including a guest suite on the ground floor. Every bedroom is a suite with its own bathroom and dressing room. Sales of the second phase opened on 1 January 2026; the developer announces deliveries from the first quarter of 2027.

Strengths

  • A private pool and an accessible roof terrace for every villa, to fit out as an outdoor lounge, summer kitchen or jacuzzi.
  • Suites only: every bedroom has its own bathroom and dressing room.
  • A gated residence, guarded 24/7, with a children's play area and a concierge service.
  • Optional, on request: solar panels, home automation, a pool heat pump, underfloor heating, turnkey furnishing.
  • The plans of both models are published, with the area of each floor.

Points to confirm

  • The legal file: the developer publishes no land title, building permit or completion guarantee. These documents must be obtained and checked before any reservation.
  • The price of the unit: only starting prices by model are published (from MAD 3,690,000 for an Oasis, from MAD 4,450,000 for an Atlas), and the French and English versions of the developer's website do not show the same figures. Ask for the second-phase price list in writing.
  • Delivery: “from the first quarter of 2027” is the developer's announcement; the binding date is the one in the contract.
  • The exact floor areas: 238 m² and 262 m² are announced, but the published areas by floor add up to 219 m² (Oasis) and 249 m² (Atlas). Ask what the headline figure includes, and for the plot area of each unit.
  • Availability: the number of villas still available in the second phase is not published.
  • The signing company: the website's legal notice names OCRE BY SASY SARL, with OCRE&HABITAT as the site's publisher; no trade register (RC) number is published.
