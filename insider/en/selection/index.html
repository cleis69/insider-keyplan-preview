What we look at in each development

For every sheet, the same items, in the same order.

    Land title, building or subdivision permit, completion or refund guarantee, then the other documents: the sheet says, item by item, what is verified and what remains to be confirmed, and when.

  • Strengths and points to confirm

    What the development offers, according to the information published about the development, and what is not yet established: unit price, delivery, floor areas, availability.

    Every sheet shows the date it was last updated and the date its figures were recorded.
