The INSIDER selection in Marrakech

The INSIDER selection brings together the off-plan developments the agency recommends in Marrakech. Each one is rated A or B on TRUST: its land title, building permit and VEFA guarantee have been verified.

What gets a development into the selection

  • An A or B rating on TRUST: a development whose land title, permit or VEFA guarantee is not verified is not presented.
  • An analysis by the agency, which also says where caution is needed.
  • A sheet kept up to date: a development whose rating changes leaves the selection.
