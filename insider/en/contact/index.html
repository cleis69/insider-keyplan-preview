Video call

An appointment can take place by video call, which is handy if you live outside Morocco or would rather not travel.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Ask for a slot

    • On WhatsApp or by email, in a few words
    • Give your availability and your time zone
    • In Marrakech? Tell us if you would rather meet in person
  2. 02

    Step 2: Get the confirmation

    • The date and time, confirmed in your time zone
    • The link to join the video call
  3. 03

    Step 3: Prepare the conversation

    • The type of property you are looking for: villa, apartment or other
    • Your approximate budget and the financing you have in mind
    • When you would like to move in or receive the keys
    • The country where you live
    • Any neighbourhoods or developments you have already spotted
  4. Illustrative photo · AZIZ KOURI / Unsplash

    • Your situation: in Morocco or abroad
    • The developments in the selection that may fit
