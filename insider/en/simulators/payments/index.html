How it is calculated
  • Off-plan, the price is paid in three stages: on reservation, during construction and on handover. The three shares add up to 100%: the construction share is what is left.
  • Construction payments are equal. In your contract, they are often called “payment calls”.
  • Each amount is rounded to the dirham; any difference goes on the last payment, so that the total is exactly the price.
  • With your dates, construction payments fall at regular intervals: an assumption. The contract sets the actual due dates, often tied to construction stages.
  • Starting split: the development's published schedule when it follows this pattern; otherwise an example, to be replaced by the one in your contract.
  • No rate, no costs and no currency conversion are added to the price.

Good to know: registration duty on an off-plan purchase

Good to know: registration duty on an off-plan purchase

It does not follow the rhythm of the instalments: a small fixed duty on reservation, the rest with the final sale deed.

  • Reservation and preliminary contract

    Each is registered for a fixed duty of MAD 200; the proportional duty comes with the final sale deed.

    Source: General Tax Code 2026 (new tab), CGI, art. 135-II-16°, checked on 9 October 2026

  • Final sale deed

    Registration duty of 4% of the price stated in the deed, VAT included, plus 2% on any part paid in cash or without payment references in the deed when the price exceeds MAD 300,000 (deeds drawn up since 1 July 2026).

    Source: General Tax Code 2026 (new tab), CGI, art. 133-I-F-1°, checked on 9 October 2026

    Source: General Tax Code 2026 (new tab), CGI, art. 133-III (loi de finances n° 50-25), checked on 9 October 2026

These figures are indicative: they help you prepare your purchase, not decide it. This is neither a financing offer nor legal, tax or financial advice: your notary, your bank and your tax adviser prevail.

The schedule that binds you is the one in your reservation contract or preliminary off-plan sale contract (Law no. 107‑12): have your notary review it before you sign.
