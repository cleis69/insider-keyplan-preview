Off-plan villa in Marrakech: reading the file before you reserve

Updated 9 October 2026

Before reserving an off-plan villa in Marrakech, read three documents together: the plans, the specification and the contract, which should state the delivery schedule. INSIDER Properties only offers developments rated A or B on TRUST.

An off-plan villa is bought before it exists. This type of sale, known as VEFA (sale of a property yet to be completed), is governed in Morocco by law 107-12. In practice, the documents the developer gives you describe what you will receive: this page explains how to read them.

Reading an off-plan villa file, in order

  1. The plans. Find where the villa sits on its plot, its built area and its plot area, and compare them with the brochure.
  2. The specification. It details the materials, fittings and finishes. Compare it with the brochure: anything promised in one should appear in the other.
  3. The outdoor areas. Pool, garden, boundary walls, utility connections: note what is included in the price and what costs extra.
  4. The schedule. The delivery date, any intermediate milestones, and what happens if it is missed: ask for it all in writing.
  5. The payments. The payment schedule: how much, when, and which stage of the works each payment relates to.
  6. The notary's review. Before you sign anything, have the contract and its annexes read by the notary: legal questions go to the notary.

Questions to put to the developer about the villa itself

  • What are the plot area and the built area of this villa, and how are they measured?
  • Are the garden and the pool delivered finished, or optional?
  • Can the plans be adapted (partitions, layout), and on what terms?
  • Who will maintain the roads, lighting and shared areas of the subdivision after handover, and at what cost?
  • How does the acceptance visit work, and what does the contract provide for clearing snags?

INSIDER Properties is an agency, not a law firm: legal questions are settled with the notary.
