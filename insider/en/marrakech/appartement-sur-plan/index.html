In short

(01)

Buying an off-plan apartment in Marrakech means choosing one specific unit in a residence that does not exist yet: before reserving, compare the unit plan, the shared areas promised and the expected charges.

In a residence you are not choosing just a unit: you are joining a building, with its shared areas, its facilities and its future running costs. Off plan, all of that is judged from documents, and off-plan sales (VEFA) are governed in Morocco by law 107-12.

Choosing a unit, point by point

(02)
  1. 01

    Step 1: Start from the use

    • Live in it, rent it out, keep a pied-à-terre: the number of rooms, the floor, the aspect and the timeline follow from that choice.
  2. 02

    Step 2: Read the unit plan

    • Stated floor area, orientation, floor, what it looks onto: check that the unit number appears on the plan attached to the contract.
  3. Illustrative photo · Big Stubby / Unsplash
    03

    Step 3: Compare on the same criteria

    • For each residence, note the same items (floor area, floor, parking, shared areas, delivery date) and set them side by side rather than comparing prices alone.
  4. 04

    Step 4: Find out how the building will run

    • Expected charges, who will manage the building after handover, the residence's rules.
  5. 05

    Step 5: Have it reviewed before signing

    • Have the contract and its annexes read by the notary before you commit: legal questions go to the notary.

Questions to put to the developer about the unit

(03)
  • What is the unit number, and is the plan attached to the contract really the plan of this unit?
  • How is the stated floor area measured (internal area, with or without terrace)?
  • Are the shared areas and facilities promised (lift, pool, gardens) in the specification, or only in the brochure?
  • What charges are expected, and who will manage the building after handover?
  • Are parking, cellar or terrace included in the price, or sold separately?
  • What delivery date is written into the contract, and what does it provide for in case of delay?

INSIDER Properties is an agency, not a law firm: legal questions are settled with the notary.
