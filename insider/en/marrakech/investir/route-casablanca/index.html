Investing on the Route de Casablanca (Marrakech)

Page updated on 9 October 2026

The Route de Casablanca leaves Marrakech heading north, towards Casablanca. If you travel regularly between the two cities, time how long it takes to reach this road from the development.

The area at a glance

This road leaves Marrakech heading north, towards Casablanca. The character of the area changes with the distance from the centre: drive through it, at several times of day, before you decide.

What matters for an investment

For an investment project, look at the future surroundings of the development: what is already built around it and what is still to come. Ask the developer to show you on a plan, then go and see for yourself.

What to check on site before reserving

  • How long it takes to reach the road out towards Casablanca from the development, if you travel regularly.
  • The immediate surroundings: vacant plots, businesses, traffic.
  • The distance to everyday services: shops, healthcare, schools.
