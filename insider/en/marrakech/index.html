In short

(01)

Off plan, you buy a file before you buy walls.

In Marrakech, as elsewhere in Morocco, a new development can be sold before it is built: this is the off-plan sale (VEFA), governed by law 107-12. The plans, the specification and the contract describe what you will receive.

INSIDER Properties guides that purchase from the first video call to the handover of the keys: choosing the development, reservation, signing at the notary's, following the works.

Whether you live in Morocco or abroad, the first conversation takes place by video call or on WhatsApp, in French or in English.
